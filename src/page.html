Good to know

How do I add SpaceX launches to Google Calendar?
Pick a calendar above and press "Add to Google Calendar", then confirm. It appears in your calendar list on the web, Android and iPhone, and updates on its own.
How do I add them to my iPhone, Apple Calendar or Outlook?
Press "Apple / Outlook" to subscribe directly, or copy the ICS link. On iPhone: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar. In Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
Can I follow only Starship or crewed launches?
Yes. Besides every SpaceX launch, there are calendars for Starship flights, for crewed Dragon missions, and for everything except routine Starlink launches.
How fresh is it?
Launch data is checked every two hours. Google calendars update right away; Apple and Outlook refresh on their own schedule, usually a few times a day.
Why are some launches all-day or marked (TBD)?
When only the day is known, the launch is an all-day event. "(TBD)" means the date is not confirmed yet. Launches known only to the month are left out until they firm up.
Will it fill my calendar with alerts?
No. Events show as free time and have no reminders of their own; add one to any launch you want to watch.
How do I unsubscribe?
Remove the calendar in your calendar app, like any other subscribed calendar.
Where does the launch schedule come from?
From Launch Library 2 by The Space Devs, a community-run database of launches. launchcal is not affiliated with SpaceX.
